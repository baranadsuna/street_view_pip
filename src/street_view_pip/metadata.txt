about=Street View Navigator (PiP) brings seamless Google Maps Pegman workflow directly into the QGIS Map Canvas. Activate or drag the yellow Pegman handle onto any satellite basemap or vector layer in any CRS (including EPSG:5253 TUREF / TM30) to toggle live Street View blue coverage lines and open a crisp, resizable bottom-right Picture-in-Picture (PiP) Street View viewport powered by hardware-accelerated Chromium WebView2 (Windows) or QtWebEngine (QGIS 4 / Linux / macOS). As you pan 360 degrees or walk along streets inside Street View, the Pegman pin and directional Field-of-View (FOV) cone synchronize live on your QGIS canvas. Includes Clean Panorama Mode, S/M/L/Max presets, corner drag resizing, optional Google Maps JS API / Panoramax support, and 1-click EPSG:5253 field observation point logging for urban planning surveys.
